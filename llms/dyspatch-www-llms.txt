# Dyspatch

## About Dyspatch

Dyspatch empowers your team to create, innovate, and grow conversations with your customers globally. Our mission is to revolutionize how global teams create personalized communications - from emails to SMS, push, and voice notifications - all without writing code.

Dyspatch is more than an email builder, it’s the centralized platform where all your communication comes together to unleash creativity and strategy. By streamlining the creation process of email, SMS, push, and voice notifications, your team can deepen personalization, improve brand consistency, and scale your business globally. Dyspatch empowers teams of all sizes to craft stunning, high performing emails without writing code.

- Support for both technical and non-technical users
- Flexibility without template lock-in
- Control, collaboration, and streamlined deployment

With Dyspatch, you can create personalized, data-driven experiences for your customers and unleash growth and retention. As enterprises scale globally, managing multiple channels efficiently becomes critical - this is where Dyspatch excels. Our intelligent solution integrates email, SMS, push, and voice notifications while maintaining perfect brand consistency and compliance. We understand enterprise challenges: complex workflows, strict guidelines, and the need for speed without compromising quality. That's why we've built robust features for dynamic content, personalization, and cross-channel analytics that streamline production and drive results. With Dyspatch, teams can orchestrate sophisticated customer journeys, optimize resources through centralized asset management, and accelerate time-to-market for global campaigns. Partner with us to elevate your enterprise strategy and unlock growth through more efficient, engaging customer connections.


## Key Features

- **No Code Email Builder**: Dyspatch offers an intuitive, drag-and-drop builder that makes it easy to create both transactional and marketing emails. It also supports SMS, push, and voice notifications, giving teams flexibility across channels without needing to code

## Who Uses Dyspatch?

Dyspatch is the customer communications platform that global brands love. From email to push and SMS, Dyspatch makes personalization and collaboration a cinch. No coding knowledge required! Global brands like Canva, Vetcove, Healthylife, Coda, Culture Amp, and more use Dyspatch to create and collaborate on their communications

## Use Cases

- **Email Builder**: Build and collaborate on your Transactional then easily export them to your CRM or ESP with Dyspatch. 
- **SMS and Push notifications**: Build and collaborate on customized SMS and Push notification campaigns in Dyspatch.
- **Customer Profiles**: Built and test your complex personalized email content with Dyspatch’s Customer profiles and visual code builder. Test all use cases quickly and easily without having to export to your ESP for testing. 
- **Localizations and Email Translation**: Translate your emails with one click using our built in integration support for XTM International, Crowdin, Phrase, Phrase Strings, Smartling, and built-in AI translations. 
- **Integration support**: Build your marketing and transactional emails in dispatch, then export them to your sending platform of choice. Dyspatch supports popular ESPs and CRMS, such as: Salesforce Marketing Cloud, HubSpot, Marketo, Iterable, Braze, Sendgrid, Sendpulse, Sparkpost, and more!
- **Email Guardrails**: Never send a broken email again with Despatch’s built in Email Quality Assurance, and pre-send checklist. 
- **Email Design**: Design your emails in Figma, Photoshop and more, then import your email design system into dyspatch for easy implementation.
